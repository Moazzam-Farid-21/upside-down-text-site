Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

What this site collects

The text you type into the generator is processed entirely in your browser. It is never sent to, or stored on, our servers. We don't ask you to create an account or give us any personal information to use the tool. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and we use them only to reply.

Hosting and server logs

This site is delivered through Cloudflare. Like most web hosts, Cloudflare may process technical request data, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use this information to identify individual visitors.

Browser storage

If you switch between light and dark mode, the site saves that choice in your browser's local storage so it can remember it on your next visit. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your site data in your browser settings.

Advertising

This site does not currently display ads. We may apply to show ads served by Google AdSense in the future. If ads are switched on, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and you can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings. If that happens, we will update this page first and provide any consent choices that are required where you live.

Analytics

This site does not currently use a separate analytics service. If that changes, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and why.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings, and you can use the tool without providing any personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "last updated" date above shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@upsidedowntext.design or through the contact page.
