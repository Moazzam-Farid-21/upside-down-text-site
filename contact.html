Contact

Questions, bug reports and corrections are all welcome. You can reach us at:

hello@upsidedowntext.design

What to include

  • Bug reports: the text you typed, which tab you used, and the device, browser or app where the output looked wrong.
  • Corrections to a guide: the page address and what you believe is inaccurate.
  • Missing characters or ideas: tell us what you were trying to create and what the tool produced instead.
  • Privacy questions: see the privacy policy first, then write to us if you still have a question.

Response time

This is a small independent site, so replies can take a few days. We read every message and fix confirmed problems as soon as we can.
