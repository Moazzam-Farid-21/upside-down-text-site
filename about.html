About this site

Upside Down Text Generator is a free tool that turns normal text into upside down, reversed, mirrored and decorative Unicode text that you can copy and paste anywhere. There is no account, no app to install and nothing to pay.

How the tool works

Everything runs in your browser. When you type, the page swaps each character for a lookalike Unicode character, mostly taken from the International Phonetic Alphabet and a few math and symbol blocks. The text you type is not sent to our servers or stored. You can read more in the Unicode explainer, and the full set of characters is on the upside down alphabet chart.

What this tool cannot do

Flipped text is a visual trick built from existing characters, not a font or a setting. Some capital letters and symbols have no true upside down twin and stay unchanged. Some older apps cannot draw certain characters and show an empty box, and some platforms limit special characters in usernames. The guides on this site describe these limits as general patterns, because apps change their rules over time and we have not tested every app version.

How the guides are written

The guides explain how flipped text behaves and where it tends to work or fail. Examples in them are generated with the same tool you can use on the homepage. Where a statement depends on a specific app, we describe it as a general pattern rather than a guarantee. If something in a guide is wrong or out of date, please tell us and we will correct it.

How the site is funded

The site is free to use. It may show ads in the future to cover hosting and the time it takes to maintain it. Our privacy policy explains what data is and is not collected, and any ads will be kept away from the tool's controls so they do not get in the way.

Who runs it
